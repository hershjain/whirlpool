Summaries, tags and answers are generated by a language model. They are frequently useful and occasionally wrong — a summary may misstate what an article said, and an answer may be confidently incorrect. Treat them as a convenience for finding your way back to the original, not as a substitute for reading it, and don't rely on them for anything that matters medically, legally or financially.
